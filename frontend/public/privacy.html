Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SummAI ("SummAI", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, processes, and protects information when users access the SummAI web application and browser extension.

1. About SummAI

SummAI is an AI-powered productivity tool that helps users:

  • Generate summaries from selected text and documents.
  • Create flowcharts from educational and research content.
  • Generate structured learning roadmaps.
  • Improve reading, research, and learning productivity.

2. Information We Collect

SummAI only processes information that users explicitly choose to submit.

This may include:

  • User-selected webpage text.
  • Text entered manually into the application.
  • PDF documents uploaded by users.
  • Technical information required to process requests.

SummAI does not intentionally collect:

  • Passwords.
  • Credit card or banking information.
  • Government-issued identification numbers.
  • Personal contacts.
  • Browsing history unrelated to user requests.

3. How We Use Information

Information submitted through SummAI is used solely to:

  • Generate AI-powered summaries.
  • Create flowcharts.
  • Generate learning roadmaps.
  • Provide requested AI outputs.
  • Improve reliability and performance of the service.

4. Data Processing

User-submitted content may be transmitted to SummAI backend services and AI processing providers for the sole purpose of generating the requested output.

SummAI does not use submitted content for advertising purposes.

5. Data Sharing

SummAI does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only:

  • With cloud infrastructure providers required to operate the service.
  • With AI service providers used to generate requested outputs.
  • When required by law or legal process.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide requested services.
  • Maintain security and reliability.
  • Troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

7. Security

SummAI uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

8. User Control

Users remain in control of the information they submit. The browser extension processes webpage content only when a user explicitly selects text or initiates a SummAI action.

9. Children's Privacy

SummAI is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
